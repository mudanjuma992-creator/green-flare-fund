# Green Flare Fund

Green Flare Fund is a decentralized social impact platform that revolutionizes volunteer coordination through an innovative "Impact Mining" system. Community members earn Green Flare Tokens (GFT) by completing verified volunteer activities, with rewards dynamically calculated based on measurable environmental and social outcomes. The platform creates a self-sustaining ecosystem where volunteer contributions are recognized, tracked, and rewarded through blockchain technology.

The platform employs a sophisticated three-tier verification system combining self-reporting, peer validation, and third-party NGO confirmation to ensure authentic impact measurement. Smart contracts utilize a Proof-of-Impact consensus mechanism where experienced volunteers stake reputation tokens to verify contributions, while IoT sensor integration provides real-time data feeds for autonomous verification of environmental projects. The unique "Impact Ripple" feature tracks cascading positive effects throughout communities, rewarding volunteers for both direct actions and inspiring others.

Local communities can leverage accumulated tokens to propose and vote on initiatives, with funding automatically released from community treasuries as verified milestones are achieved. The platform provides corporations with verified carbon credits and ESG compliance solutions while maintaining the core principle that volunteer work remains unpaid but empowered through tokenized governance. This creates sustainable value for volunteers, communities, and corporate partners seeking measurable social and environmental impact.
